**"remember dont show to much things outside be simple and important"** (owner, 16 Nov 2026),
said while removing the "Billing period" column from admin Plan Payment — it printed
`summary.periods` as "14 periods" while the Status column printed the same number again
inside "14 of 14 unpaid".

**Why:** every extra column costs width the important ones need. On that table the squeeze
broke the header mid-word ("Amou nt (RM)") and the figure mid-number ("1,750.0 0"). The
column that went is the one that said nothing about what needs doing.

**How to apply:** before adding a column, cell or strip, ask what DECISION it changes. If
another element on the row already carries the fact, drop it rather than show it twice.
Prefer the wording that names the action over the wording that names the quantity. See
[[innocenz-status-colour-code]] and [[kpi-formula-never-displayed]] — same instinct: give
the reader the conclusion, not the working.
